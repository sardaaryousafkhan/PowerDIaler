Operator help

Short, honest notes for running DashDialer locally or with your company account. This page ships with the app — it is not a marketing site.

Command palette & search

Press ⌘K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (Windows/Linux), or click “Search or jump…” in the top bar. Jump to Dashboard, Campaigns, Members, Billing, User portal, and more. Typing a campaign name filters live campaign results.

Theme

Use the theme control in the top command bar (right side, next to Help and notifications). Choices: System, Dark, Light. The setting is saved to your account preferences when signed in.

Importing contacts

Open a campaign → Import. Phone numbers must be valid E.164 (example: +12025550101). Many fake +1555… patterns are rejected. Fix invalid rows and re-import.

SMS duplicates

The dialer skips sending the same SMS to a contact again inside a short window. On the dashboard those show as skipped (already texted), not as failures. That is intentional anti-spam behavior, not a bug.

Zoom Phone / dialing

Voice campaigns can open a Zoom Phone click-to-call URL for the current contact when Zoom is configured in Integrations. In demo mode, calls are simulated — no real carrier traffic.

Demo mode

With DEMO_MODE=1 on the API, Redis workers are not required; calls and SMS are simulated so you can click through the product safely.
