Common questions

How do I sign in?

Use the campus email and password issued by your college or university. Password resets are handled through your campus inbox — not inside the App.

QR attendance is not working

Make sure camera permission is enabled for Campus Desk. Hold the QR code inside the frame in good lighting. If the session has expired, ask your instructor for a fresh code.

I don’t see my courses or news

Course and news content is published by your institution. Contact your campus IT or registrar if your schedule looks incorrect.

How do I update my student record?

Profile and admissions details are managed by your university. Reach out to your campus administrator for changes to name, program, or enrollment status.

Push notifications

Enable notifications in Campus Desk settings and in your device Settings → Campus Desk. You can turn them off at any time.
