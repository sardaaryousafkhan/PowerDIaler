Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026 · Derived Studio

Campus Desk (“the App”) is published by Derived Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information the App processes, why we process it, and the choices available to students and participating institutions.

1. Who this applies to

Campus Desk is a student portal for colleges and universities. Accounts are issued by your institution. This policy covers students, faculty, and staff who sign in with a campus email provided by their school.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — campus email address, student ID, name, program, and role (for example, student or faculty) as supplied by your institution.
  • Admissions & record data — admission status, enrollment details, and profile fields shown in your student record.
  • Attendance data — class or lab check-ins created when you scan a QR code, including session time, course, and section.
  • Course & schedule data — syllabi, course lists, and term filters synced from your institute.
  • Campus news — announcements and notices published by your institution that appear in the App feed.
  • Device permissions — camera access is used only for QR attendance scanning. Push notification tokens are stored if you enable alerts.
  • Support communications — messages you send to us when requesting help.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your signed-in session.
  • Record and display attendance for classes and labs.
  • Show courses, news, and campus announcements relevant to you.
  • Send optional push notifications for important campus alerts.
  • Respond to support requests and fix technical issues.
  • Comply with lawful requests from your institution where applicable.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in Campus Desk.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites for marketing.
  • We do not access your camera except when you actively scan a QR code for attendance.

5. Sharing with third parties

We share data only with your educational institution (the data controller for student records), infrastructure providers that host the App under contract, and authorities when required by law. We do not share student data with advertisers.

6. Data retention

Account and attendance records are retained according to your institution’s policies and our agreement with them. You may request deletion through your campus administrator or by contacting us at the address below.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and minimal data collection by design. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; report concerns to us promptly.

8. Children’s privacy

Campus Desk is intended for enrolled students at participating institutions. Accounts are provisioned by the college or university, not opened directly by minors without institutional oversight.

9. Your choices

  • Disable push notifications in App settings or your device settings.
  • Contact your institution to update or correct student record information.
  • Email us to exercise privacy rights available in your region.

10. International users

If you use Campus Desk outside your institution’s primary country, data may be processed where our servers and your institution’s systems operate. We apply appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
