# DashDialer — customer help

## Install

1. Download the Mac or Windows installer from [derivedstudio.com/dashdialer](https://derivedstudio.com/dashdialer/).
2. Open the installer and follow the prompts.
3. Launch **DashDialer**.

## Activate

1. Sign in (or use the account your seller set up).
2. Open **License** in the sidebar.
3. Paste the key you received (looks like `DD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
4. Press **Activate**.

## If features are locked

You will see a banner: “License inactive.”

- Check that your PC is online and try **Refresh status**.
- Contact Derived Studio to renew or re-issue a key.
- If you replaced a computer, ask them to **clear devices** on your license (limit is usually 2 PCs).

## Offline

After a successful activation, the app can keep working offline for up to **7 days**. After that it must check the license online again.

## What stays on your computer

Campaigns, contacts, and message history stay in local app data on your machine. The website only answers “is this license still paid?”
